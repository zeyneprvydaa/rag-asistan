What is the National Technology Initiative?
The National Technology Initiative is a comprehensive development vision aimed at making Turkey a fully independent, prosperous, and high-technology-producing country. It aims to build a sustainable ecosystem in high technology, innovation, digital transformation, entrepreneurship, and human capital.

What is TEKNOFEST Aerospace and Technology Festival?
TEKNOFEST is the world's largest aerospace, space, and technology festival, organized with the participation of institutions and organizations that contribute to the development of national technology in Turkey.

What is the primary objective of TEKNOFEST?
Its primary objective is to increase public interest in technology, encourage young people to produce technology, and contribute to the goal of "A Turkey That Produces Technology" in line with the National Technology Initiative.

Which fields does TEKNOFEST cover?
It covers many technological and scientific fields, primarily aviation and space, artificial intelligence, software, robotic systems, energy, environment, agriculture, health, and maritime technologies.

When is TEKNOFEST held?
It is held on dates determined every year in different cities and countries. Event schedules and dates are announced on the official website and social media accounts.

Is there an admission fee for the festival?
Participation in TEKNOFEST as a visitor is completely free of charge.

How can I attend TEKNOFEST as a visitor?
You can attend by creating an online registration on the official website or mobile application prior to the festival and entering with the generated QR code.

How is material support provided for projects?
Material support and transportation/accommodation facilities are provided to teams that pass the pre-evaluation and qualify for the finals, within the framework of the criteria specified in the specifications.

Where can I access report templates?
Report templates can be downloaded from the specifications and documents page of the relevant competition.
